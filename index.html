Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking flights, placing bids, and our loyalty program.

Our smart bidding feature lets travelers submit a custom price offer on selected flights and cabin classes. Participating airlines review open bids leading up to departure. If your offer meets the airline's acceptance threshold, your ticket is automatically confirmed and issued at your bid price!

You automatically earn miles every time you book through the Maestro Aero app. Points are calculated based on the actual flight distance and class of travel. You can redeem points during checkout to pay for tickets, seat upgrades, baggage allowances, or partner hotel perks.

Yes! Maestro Aero offers direct web and mobile check-in starting 24 to 48 hours prior to scheduled departure. Once checked in, your digital boarding pass with barcoded verification will be available offline in your app's "My Pocket" section.

We support all major international and domestic credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), mobile financial services (bKash, Nagad), and digital wallets. All transactions are protected by bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliance.

Refund and change rules vary depending on the fare conditions set by the specific airline (e.g. Non-Refundable vs Flexible fares). You can review individual cancellation terms prior to booking. For detailed terms, please visit our dedicated Terms & Conditions page.
